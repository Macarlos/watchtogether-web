Last updated: November 21, 2026

What Watch2Night is

Watch2Night is a free tool that helps you find something to watch. You pick your streaming platforms and preferred mood, swipe through suggestions, and save the ones you like. It works in your browser at watch2night.com and as an Android app. There's no account and no sign-up.

By using Watch2Night, you accept these terms. If you don't agree with them, please don't use the site or the app.

Local storage & anonymous statistics

Watch2Night doesn't use analytics platforms (like Google Analytics) and doesn't sell your data. We store a few things in the local storage of your browser or app (similar to cookies; it stays on your device):

  • Your saved picks and the titles you marked as watched — so they're still there next time. You can clear them anytime via "Clear all picks" in the app, or in your browser's or app's site-data settings.
  • Small settings — for example whether you've closed the cookie notice and the intro tour.
  • A random anonymous ID, created on your device. It is not derived from your name, an account, or any hardware or advertising identifier. It is used only to count how many different devices use Watch2Night and how many of them come back.
  • In the Android app: when your ad-free period ends (see "Advertising & affiliate links").

Saved picks and settings are strictly necessary for features you use directly. Under GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive, this kind of essential functional storage doesn't require opt-in consent, only disclosure, which is what this section is. The anonymous ID is used only for our own aggregate usage statistics.

On our server we keep simple usage statistics: how many times Watch2Night was opened (and on which platform, such as the website or the Android app), which platforms and genres are chosen most, and, for the anonymous ID, a scrambled (hashed) version of it, the day it was first seen, the days it was used in the last 60 days, and the country it connected from. To work out the country, your IP address is sent to a geolocation service (freeipapi.com); we use only the country and don't store your IP address in these statistics. Our hosting provider (Render) may keep standard server logs, which can include IP addresses, for a limited time. None of this is used to identify you.

Advertising & affiliate links

Watch2Night is free to use, and running it has real costs. To help pay for it, it shows ads from Google:

  • On the website: ads from Google AdSense.
  • In the Android app: ads from Google AdMob — a banner at the bottom of the screen, plus an optional rewarded video: if you choose to watch one short video, the app hides ads for 24 hours.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, your device's advertising ID and similar technologies to show and measure ads and, where you agree, to personalise them. They do this under Google's own privacy policy, and Google is an independent controller of that data. We don't sell your data. Read more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — and wherever else the law requires it — Google's consent message asks for your choice before ads that use personal data are shown. You can change your choice at any time (see "Your choices").

We may also add affiliate links in the future — for example, earning a small commission if you rent, buy or subscribe through a link shown in the app, at no extra cost to you. If that happens, we'll mark those links clearly and update this page first.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: in the Android app, where it applies, an "Ad privacy settings" link appears in the footer so you can review or change your choice. On the website, use Google's consent message when it is shown, or your browser's cookie settings.
  • Personalised ads: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation. On the web, see adssettings.google.com.
  • No ads for 24 hours: in the Android app, tap the offer above the banner and watch one short video.
  • Delete your local data: clear the site data of your browser or the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Your anonymous ID is then removed from your device and a new random one is created if you use Watch2Night again.
  • Questions or requests: write to feedback@watch2night.com.

Languages & translation

Watch2Night's interface is available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Hindi, and Polish, automatically shown based on your detected location.

For movie and show descriptions: English, Spanish, French, and German come directly from our data provider's own catalog. Portuguese, Hindi, and Polish descriptions are machine-translated using a third-party AI service (Groq), since our data provider doesn't offer these languages natively. Machine translation can occasionally be imperfect — the original English description is always the authoritative source if anything reads oddly.

Shared picks links

If you use the "Share my picks" feature, Watch2Night generates a link containing only the internal catalog IDs of the titles you liked — no personal information, no names, no identifying data of any kind. Anyone who opens that link can see which titles were included; nothing else about you is shared or stored.

Where the data comes from

Movie and TV information — titles, posters, descriptions, ratings, and streaming availability — is provided by the Streaming Availability API by Movie of the Night, a third-party data provider, via their API. Watch2Night does not own, verify, or guarantee the accuracy of this data. Trailer links point to a YouTube search for the title, not a specific verified video.

Availability and pricing is shown for one of twenty supported countries — United States, Canada, India, United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, Mexico, Spain, Portugal, Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, France, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Poland, Singapore, or the United Arab Emirates — automatically detected from your connection. If you're browsing from anywhere else, you'll see the US catalog by default. If your detected country is wrong (for example, due to a VPN) or you're located elsewhere, what you see may not match what's actually offered on your local version of these platforms. Always confirm current availability and price directly on the streaming platform before making a purchase or rental decision.
